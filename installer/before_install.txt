DiscordAnt reads Discord the way a person does: it opens a browser, you log in, and it scrolls and saves what it sees.

Please know this before you install:

- Discord's terms do not allow automating a personal account. DiscordAnt goes slowly on purpose, but nobody can promise that an account will not be limited or closed for using it. You use it at your own risk.

- It only collects what your own account can already see. Treat other people's messages with care, and follow the rules of the servers you are in.

- What it collects stays on this PC. DiscordAnt has no account, no server and no tracking.

- DiscordAnt is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord.
